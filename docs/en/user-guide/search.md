# Search

Search helps you locate resources that your account can access. Results may include Protocols, Records, discussions, Projects, or other indexed objects depending on the Platform version and deployment configuration.

## Search effectively

- Start with a distinctive Protocol, Project, sample, or field term.
- Narrow the scope to a Lab or Project when the interface offers filters.
- Use stable IDs when names are ambiguous.
- Check the result’s Lab, Project, Protocol version, recorder, and date before using it.

Search results reflect the permissions evaluated for your account. A resource that another person can find may be absent from your results because it is private, belongs to another scope, or has not yet been indexed.

## When a result is missing

1. Open the expected Lab or Project directly and confirm your membership.
2. Check spelling, identifiers, filters, and time range.
3. Confirm that the resource was saved and has not been archived or deleted.
4. Ask the resource owner to verify access rather than requesting a duplicate export.

Search is a discovery aid, not a substitute for a governed inventory or backup. Use approved exports and operator backups for retention requirements.
